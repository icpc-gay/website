01 / What is ICPC.GAY?

A place made by programmers, for programmers.

ICPC.GAY is an independent community for people who love competitive programming, software, and building things.

02 / Why .GAY (Fun)?

Because programmers are allowed to have fun too.

ICPC.GAY isn't affiliated with ICPC. The name is simply a playful way of saying that we're a bunch of people who are way too passionate about competitive programming.

03 / What we believe

Code is more than solving problems.

It's about curiosity.
It's about making things.
It's about learning from failure.
It's about staying up late because you really want to know why it works.

Competitive programming is only part of the story.

04 / Who is it for?

  • Beginners
  • Competitive programmers
  • Developers
  • Tinkerers
  • Anyone who enjoys code
